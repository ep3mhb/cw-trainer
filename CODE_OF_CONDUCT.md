# Code of conduct

## The short version

This is a project for teaching people Morse code. Be patient with
beginners, be straight with each other, and assume the person you are replying
to is trying to help.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing experience levels. Someone learning their first
  five characters and someone running 35 WPM are both members here.
- Keep criticism on the code and the ideas, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers have to say no to things, and that contributors have
  other commitments.
- Assume good faith before assuming carelessness.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, or in contact off the back
  of a contribution here.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Dismissing or belittling someone for being new, slow, or asking a basic
  question.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, reviews, commit
messages — and to any project space it runs, including on the air where a
contact arises from this project.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to the maintainer, Mohammad Bahrololoum,
at info@mikroutik.com. Reports are read by him alone and will be handled
discreetly.

The maintainer may warn, temporarily block, or permanently block someone from
the repository, and will tell the person reporting what was decided.
