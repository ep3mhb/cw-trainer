CW Trainer — learn Morse code in your browser

Free, nothing to install, and no account needed to start practising.

  • New to Morse: start at lesson 1 — two characters, sent at full speed and spaced wide apart, until you know them cold.
  • Coming back: take the exam for a character and skip straight past it.
  • Running a class: share one link; your students can call each other on the band and hold a real exchange.

The Koch method, sent at full character speed from the first minute with Farnsworth spacing; sending practice with your keyboard, a straight key or an iambic paddle; and live contacts with other members on a simulated 40-metre band. Your progress is kept in this browser.
