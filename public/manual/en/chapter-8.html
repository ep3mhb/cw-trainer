Chapter 8The Progress tab

8.1What is measured

Sessions / Characters copied / Overall accuracyTotals over everything you have done on the Receive tab.Characters unlockedYour course position, out of forty.Best effective speedThe highest effective speed at which you completed a session.Effective speed over timeA chart of your sessions. Two or more are needed to draw a trend.Confusion matrixA grid of what was sent (rows) against what you typed (columns). It turns “I keep making mistakes” into “I hear S as H”, which you can actually drill with “My own set”.Per-character accuracy, worst firstEach character with how often you copied it right. The top of this list is your next practice set.Session history / Sending historyEvery receive session and every analysed sending run, with date, lesson, speeds, accuracy, weighting, consistency and latency.

Sessions CSV and Per-character CSV download the tables for a spreadsheet.

Where it lives

Progress lives in this browser, on this device, at this address. It does not follow you to another browser, and clearing your browsing data removes it. Export regularly from Settings — that file is the only backup that exists, unless you have switched on training records (chapter 7), in which case a summary of each session is also kept on the server and appears here as Across your devices.
